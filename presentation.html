1. The Problem: Agronomic & Ecological Crisis

Problem Context

Excessive Over-Fertilization

Farmers traditionally broadcast blanket fertilizer doses regardless of existing soil reserves. This leads to nutrient saturation, high operational expenses, and the law of diminishing returns where additional input yields zero biological gain.

💸 High Input Cost

Nitrate Leaching & Run-Off

Excess nitrogen fertilizers dissolved in heavy rain leach into underground aquifers or wash into river basins, causing massive eutrophication, dead zones, and greenhouse nitrous oxide (N2O) emissions.

🌊 Environmental Hazard

Spatial Field Inconsistency

Single-field farms exhibit significant soil variability in pH, organic matter, and baseline nutrients. Treating whole fields uniformly leads to over-feeding rich zones and starving depleted sectors.

4. Mathematical Optimization: Mitscherlich & SLSQP

Mathematics

Mitscherlich-Baule Yield Equation

Y = Y_max · (1 - 10^(-c_n · N_tot)) · (1 - 10^(-c_p · P_tot)) · (1 - 10^(-c_k · K_tot)) · f(pH) · f(Rain)
  • • Law of Diminishing Returns: Each incremental kg of fertilizer produces progressively less yield.
  • • Total Nutrient Pool: N_tot = S_n (Soil test) + ΔN (Fertilizer).
  • • pH Lockout Factor: Bell-curve multiplier f(pH) modeling nutrient fixation outside 6.0–7.8.
  • • Precipitation Penalty: Accounts for nitrate leaching run-off.

SLSQP Constrained Optimization

minimize Cost = w_n·ΔN + w_p·ΔP + w_k·ΔK + Penalty(Leach)
subject to:
  • Y(ΔN, ΔP, ΔK) >= Y_target (Yield Guarantee)
  • 0 <= ΔN <= 150, 0 <= ΔP <= 80, 0 <= ΔK <= 90
  • • Sequential Least Squares Programming: Non-linear constraint solver.
  • • Zero-Cost Edge Case: Returns exact 0 kg/ha when soil baseline meets target.
  • • Biological Feasibility Guard: Catches unattainable yield targets.

14. Commercial Fertilizer Bags & 4R Split Schedule

Operations

Commercial 50 kg Bag Translation

Converts abstract N, P, K kilograms into standard 50 kg retail bags:

  • • Urea (46-0-0): Nitrogen top-dressing
  • • DAP (18-46-0): Diammonium Phosphate for early root establishment
  • • MOP (0-0-60): Muriate of Potash for stalk resilience

*Simultaneous Compensation: Subtracts Nitrogen delivered via DAP from Urea requirement to prevent over-fertilization.

4R Split Application Calendar

  • • Stage 1 (Basal at Sowing): 100% P (DAP) + 50% K (MOP) + 25% N (Urea)
  • • Stage 2 (Vegetative / Tillering, +28 Days): 50% N (Urea) during peak nitrogen uptake
  • • Stage 3 (Reproductive, +55 Days): 25% N + 50% K for grain filling
